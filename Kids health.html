Here are some fact about space

  • The universe is about 13.8 billion years old.
  • There are an estimated 2 trillion galaxies in the observable universe.
  • Our home galaxy is about 105,700 light-years wide

The solar system is a vast family of celestial bodies centered around the Sun, a massive star that holds over 99% of the system's total mass. Eight planets orbit the Sun in elliptical paths: the four inner rocky terrestrial planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars) and the four outer giant planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune).
